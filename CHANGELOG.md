## 1.1 — Calendar & flexible history (October 2026)

- Calendar shows scheduled vs. completed days and logged set counts.
- Assign A/B/C/D or rest to any calendar date, independent of completion.
- Log completed workouts for past dates, or plan future workouts.
- Edit completed workout dates/groups and individual sets, including reps, weight and effort.
- Add forgotten sets/cardio, update notes, and remove incorrect entries.
- Week-at-a-glance strip on Today.
- Compatible with previous v1 on-device data and JSON backups.
- Improved mobile calendar layout, still free, private and local-first.
